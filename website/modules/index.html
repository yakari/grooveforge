Built-in modules

What each rack slot type does and how it connects to the rest of the patch.

GrooveForge Keyboard

Multi-timbral SoundFont (.sf2) engine with up to 16 MIDI channels per slot, per-channel bank/program, optional per-slot vocoder, and gesture actions on the on-screen keys. Wire MIDI IN from hardware or from Virtual Piano / Looper; audio OUT goes to the master bus or other effects via patch cables.

Virtual Piano

Touch keyboard with no internal sound — it only forwards MIDI through its jacks. Ideal ahead of a SoundFont or VST instrument. Connect Scale IN from Jam Mode for highlighted scale degrees and snapping.

Vocoder (GFPA)

Dedicated slot with carrier waveforms (saw, square, choral, natural/PSOLA), MIDI pitch bend and vibrato (CC1), and microphone modulation. Multiple vocoder slots can coexist.

Stylophone & Theremin

Monophonic Stylophone strip and Theremin pad with MIDI OUT to the rest of the rack. Theremin supports CAM mode on some platforms (motion via front camera — see privacy policy).

Riff

An electric guitar simulated string by string instead of played from recordings, so it bends, mutes and rings like one. Play it from any keyboard or MIDI source: each note goes to a string a guitarist would use, and close notes take separate strings so they all ring. Switch Legato on (from a pad, or hold one sending CC 68) for hammer-ons and pull-offs on one string, or Overlap to slur only keys played overlapping. The sustain pedal rings longer than held notes (Pedal sustain), and Feedback, on a CC slider, makes held strings bloom into a harmonic as in front of an amp. The pitch wheel bends (a whole tone by default), the mod wheel adds vibrato and the sustain pedal lets released strings ring. Palm mute, Pick position, Attack, the Pickup selector (neck, middle, bridge, single coil or humbucker), Tone and Sustain all act on strings that are already ringing, so a CC mapping can put them on a pedal, a knob or a ribbon and play them like a guitarist's hands. Cable it into the Amp for crunch and distortion. Native apps only.

Jam Mode

Single-instance processor: choose a master channel; when the master plays chords or bass (configurable), target keyboards and vocoders lock to the derived scale. Includes scale type, BPM-aligned root updates, and optional pin strip under the transport. Pairs with Virtual Piano Scale IN or internal scale lock on keyboards.

MIDI Looper

Multi-track MIDI recorder/looper with overdub, per-track mute, reverse, speed, and quantisation. MIDI IN can be any upstream source; MIDI OUT feeds instruments. State is stored inside .gf projects.

Drum Generator

Transport-synced drum machine driven by .gfdrum pattern files: step grids, humanisation, swing, fills, and optional count-in. Uses General MIDI drum notes on the configured SoundFont.

Autotune

Pitch correction for a voice or any single-note line. Patch Live Input into it: it hears the note you sing and pulls it onto the nearest note of the chosen key and scale — or of a scale patched in from Xen or Jam Mode. Retune sets how fast: 0 ms is the stepped robot sound, 50 ms and up just sounds in tune. Strength sets how far, Humanize lets held notes keep their vibrato, Flex-Tune leaves slides and blue notes alone, and Transpose goes from monster to chipmunk. A live readout shows the note you sing, how many cents off it is, and where it is being pulled.

Amp

A guitar amp in one slot, in the order a guitarist plugs things in. The Drive pedal is either an Overdrive that pushes the mids and keeps the low end tight, or a rougher Distortion, with its own Tone knob. The Amp section is the preamp's Gain, from clean to crunch, and a Bass / Middle / Treble tone stack modelled on the real circuit of three classic amps (US, Brit, AC), so the knobs interact the way they do on the real thing. The Cab section shapes the sound like a 1x12, 2x12 or 4x12 speaker cabinet. Each of the three sections has its own switch, which a CC mapping can put on a footswitch or a pad. Play it from a keyboard's guitar sound, or patch a real guitar in through Live Input.

Cabinet impulse responses

Most of a guitar sound comes from the cabinet. Besides the three built-in cabinets, the Amp can play an impulse response (IR): a recording of a real cabinet and its microphone, as a small .wav file. Tap Load IR under the Amp and pick a file; the Cabinet selector switches to IR, and the IR is saved inside your project, so it travels with it. Any WAV works (16, 24 or 32-bit, mono or stereo, any sample rate); only the first 100 ms are used, which is where a cabinet's sound lives.

Free cabinet IRs to start with:

  • TONE3000: a community library with thousands of free IRs
  • Kalthallen Cabs: free and paid packs of classic cabinets
  • Celestion: a free IR from the speaker maker

Each pack has its own licence: free to play through, but usually not to share, which is why GrooveForge cannot ship them. Download them yourself from these sites.

Descriptor effects & MIDI FX

Bundled .gfpd plugins provide reverb, delay, wah, EQ, compressor, chorus, audio harmonizer, autotune, guitar amp (audio) and harmonizer, arpeggiator, transposer, chord expand, velocity curve, gate (MIDI). Custom descriptors can be loaded from disk.
