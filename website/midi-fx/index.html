MIDI FX

Modules that change the notes you play before an instrument hears them: add harmony, turn one key into a chord, play an arpeggio.

A MIDI FX makes no sound of its own. It works on the notes (MIDI) of the instrument cabled into it, and that instrument plays the result. They all work in the web demo too. You find them under Built-in MIDI FX in the + sheet.

Connect a MIDI FX

Here a Harmonizer adds two notes above every key you press on the keyboard.

  1. Tap + and pick Harmonizer.
  2. Tap the cable icon at the top to open Patch view.
  3. Long-press the keyboard's yellow MIDI OUT and drag it to the Harmonizer's MIDI IN.
  4. Tap the back arrow and play.

Each key now plays three notes. You do not cable anything out of the Harmonizer: the keyboard plays what it sends back.

  • It works on instruments that make their own sound: the keyboard with a soundfont, the Vocoder, the Stylophone and the Theremin. A keyboard set to None (MIDI only) skips MIDI FX when you play its on-screen keys.
  • To use two MIDI FX on one keyboard, cable the keyboard's MIDI OUT to each of them.
  • The power button at the top of a MIDI FX switches it off (Bypass). To flip it from a pad on your controller, see Map a knob.
  • The labels on these panels appear in English in every language.

Harmonizer

Adds up to four notes above each note you play. Voices sets how many, and each voice (V1 to V4) sets its distance in semitones, up to two octaves. The readout names the interval: +4 is a major third, +7 a perfect fifth, which is the default pair.

With Scale Lock on, harmony notes move to the nearest note of the scale your keyboard is locked to by Xen or Jam Mode, so the harmony stays in key. To set the intervals by playing, cable a keyboard's CHORD OUT into the Harmonizer's CHORD IN and hold a chord: the notes above the lowest one become the voices. For harmony on a voice or a guitar instead of on notes, use the Audio Harmonizer.

Chord Expand

Turns each key into a full chord, with the key you press as the root. Pick the Chord Type (Maj, Min, Dim, Aug, Sus2, Sus4, Dom7, Maj7, Min7, m7b5, Dim7). Spread stacks the notes Close, or moves some of them an octave up (Open, Wide). Scale Lock, on by default, moves chord notes into the locked scale.

Arpeggiator

Hold a chord and it plays the notes one after another, in time with the tempo at the top of the screen, even when the transport is stopped. Pattern sets the order (Up, Down, UpDown, DnUp, Played, Random), Division the note length (1/4 to 1/128, and triplets), Gate how long each note lasts, and Octaves whether it climbs over 1, 2 or 3 octaves.

Transposer

Moves every note up or down. Semitones goes from -24 to +24: +12 is an octave up. Use it to play a song in another key with the fingering you know.

Velocity Curve

Changes how hard or soft your playing sounds. Mode is Power or Sigmoid (two curve shapes; Amount sets how strong) or Fixed, where every note plays at the strength set by Amount. It is for a MIDI controller with touch-sensitive keys: the on-screen keys always play at the same strength.

Gate

Lets through only the notes inside a range. Min Vel and Max Vel set how soft and how hard a note may be; Min Note and Max Note set the lowest and highest key, numbered 0 to 127 (middle C is 60). For example, set Min Note to 60 and the instrument only answers middle C and above.

Microtone

Plays the notes between the piano keys. Hold two neighbouring keys and you hear one note halfway between them, made with pitch bend. It plays one note at a time. Delay is how long it waits for your other fingers before sounding (0 plays at once). Mode picks how the held keys are averaged, and Velocity takes the average strength or the last note's. Leave Bend Range on ±2 st unless the instrument it plays uses a wider pitch-bend range. To play a whole scale in tune, chords included, use Xen instead.

Xen

Locks the keyboard to a scale you choose, and retunes it for scales that use notes between the piano keys.

A new project already has a Xen module cabled to the keyboard. Switch it on with ON, then hold a key and tap a scale: the key you hold becomes the root. The steps are in Play in a scale.

  • SNAP moves keys outside the scale to the nearest note that fits. Those keys turn grey.
  • TUNE retunes keys for scales that are not played on a piano, such as a maqam or a raga. Retuned keys get a teal band and show how many cents they move.
  • FAMILY picks the tradition: Western, Maqam, Raga, Gamelan, Temperaments and more. My Scales holds the ones you build or import (Scala .scl files work).
  • The ROOT row sets the root without playing it.

Xen reaches an instrument through two purple and teal cables, also listed on its panel under SCALE LOCK and RETUNE: SCALE OUT into the keyboard's SCALE IN locks it to the scale, and TUNE OUT into TUNE IN retunes it. Patch one without the other to lock without retuning, or the reverse. The Vocoder takes both cables too.

Make your own

A MIDI FX is a .gfpd text file with type: midi_fx and a midi_nodes: section. Add yours with Load .gfpd from file… at the bottom of the + sheet. See Plugin descriptors and HOW_TO_CREATE_A_PLUGIN.md.
