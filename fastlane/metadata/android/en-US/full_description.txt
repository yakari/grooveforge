GrooveForge is a portable MIDI keyboard studio for Android. Plug in a MIDI controller, pick a soundfont, and play.

It began as a practice companion: something light enough to carry on a phone or tablet, used to rebuild scale knowledge and muscle memory at a keyboard. It grew well beyond that, but the focus remains — a tool for practising and playing, not a DAW.

Instruments

GrooveForge Keyboard — soundfont synth (.sf2) with bank and patch selection and MIDI channel routing. Every slot is independent.
Vocoder — mic-driven voice synthesis.
Stylophone — monophonic metal-strip instrument.
Theremin — touch pad with vertical pitch and horizontal volume.
Drum Generator — beat patterns from bossa nova to metal, with human feel.

Xen: microtonal scales

Hold a note, tap a scale. The note you are holding becomes the tonic, the keyboard locks to the scale built on it, and — for scales that are not equal-tempered — the keys it uses are retuned to sound as the tradition intends. One keyboard, one gesture.

92 scales across eleven families: Western modes; Arabic and Turkish maqamat; Persian dastgah; Indian ragas in just intonation; Chinese, Japanese and Korean modes; Thai seven-equal; Javanese and Balinese gamelan; Ethiopian qenet and Ugandan amadinda; Highland pipe, hardanger fiddle and Byzantine chant; historical temperaments; and equal divisions from 13 to 31 tones, Bohlen-Pierce, and Wendy Carlos's non-octave alpha, beta and gamma.

Retuning uses a full MIDI tuning table rather than pitch bend, so every note of a chord sounds at its own correct pitch — pitch bend is per channel and can only move one note at a time. On the keyboard, out-of-scale keys grey out and retuned ones carry their offset in cents.

Each scale says where its numbers come from: a temperament or an equal division is exact, a maqam is written in the convention that notates the practice, a gamelan or an Ethiopian qenet is tuned by ear and differs from one instrument to the next.

Build your own scales too — any number of degrees, an offset per key, and a mute that keeps a degree playable while the scale lock ignores it. Import and export Scala (.scl) files.

Loopers

MIDI Looper — record and loop MIDI patterns with bar sync.
Audio Looper — record and loop live audio with bar sync, overdub and reverse.

Effects

Plate reverb, ping-pong delay, auto-wah, 4-band EQ, compressor and chorus/flanger, with BPM sync where it makes sense. Live Input routes a microphone or line-in through any of them.

And the rest

Plugin rack — an ordered, drag-and-drop rack of module slots, reorderable at any time.
Scale lock and highlighting — root, in-scale and out-of-scale keys coloured on the on-screen keyboard.
Jam Mode — one slot leads; the slots after it lock to its harmony.
CC mapping — map hardware knobs, sliders and pads to MIDI effects or application actions.
Projects — save and restore complete rack configurations to a .gf file. The last session is autosaved on every change.

GrooveForge is free software, built by one developer in their spare time. Rough edges are expected, and releases happen when time allows.
