Added
- Both harmonizers: four voices, and a CHORD IN jack — the chord you play sets the intervals.
- Audio Harmonizer: Scale Lock bends each voice to the locked scale.
- Vocoder: a new Harmony mode that sings the chord back in your own voice.
- 12 drum styles: techno, electro, dub, R&B, Arabic, Turkish, Persian, Indian, taiko and luogu.

Fixed
- Audio Harmonizer: crackling, aliasing and uneven voice levels.
- Android: silence and lag with USB-C DACs and headphones.
