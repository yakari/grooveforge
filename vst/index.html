VST3 plugins

Use the VST3 instruments and effects installed on your computer as modules in the rack. Desktop only: Linux, macOS and Windows.

A project that contains a VST3 plugin still opens on a phone. The plugin's module shows This VST3 plugin is not available on mobile. and stays silent.

Load a plugin

Add an installed instrument or effect to the rack.

  1. Tap + and scroll to VST3 Plugins.
  2. Tap Pick Installed Instrument or Pick Installed Effect, then pick a plugin from the list.
  3. Not in the list? Tap Browse VST3 Instrument… or Browse VST3 Effect… and select the plugin's .vst3 folder.

The plugin appears in the rack as a module. An instrument gets a MIDI CH badge; an effect does not.

The list only shows plugins placed directly in these folders. For a plugin in a subfolder, use Browse.

SystemFolders searched
Linux~/.vst3, /usr/lib/vst3, /usr/local/lib/vst3
macOS~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
WindowsC:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

Play a VST3 instrument

Send it notes, like any other instrument.

  • From a MIDI controller: set the module's MIDI CH badge to the channel your controller sends on.
  • From the on-screen keyboard: in Patch view, cable the keyboard's MIDI OUT to the plugin's MIDI IN. The guide shows the same thing with Riff: Play the guitar from the keyboard.

Plugins follow the tempo and play/stop of the transport bar, so tempo-synced delays and arpeggiators stay in time.

Put an effect after an instrument

Run any instrument, built-in or VST3, through a VST3 effect.

  1. Load the effect (see above).
  2. In Patch view, cable the instrument's AUDIO OUT L and R into the effect's AUDIO IN L and R.

You hear the instrument through the effect.

On a VST3 instrument there is a shortcut: tap FX at the bottom of its module, then Add effect. You pick the effect's .vst3 folder and the cables are drawn for you.

Change the plugin's settings

  • In the module, tap PARAMETERS to unfold it, then tap a group to open its knobs. A search field helps with plugins that have hundreds of settings.
  • On Linux and macOS, tap Show Plugin UI to open the plugin's own window.

On Linux, some plugins draw their window with OpenGL and fail to open under Wayland. The app then says so. Start GrooveForge with LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE=1, or in an X11 session, and try again.

Saving the project keeps each plugin and the values of its parameters.

What does not work yet

  • Pitch bend, the mod wheel, aftertouch and other controller messages (CC) reach a VST3 instrument when the plugin assigns them to one of its parameters, as most synths do. The bend range is the plugin's own setting.
  • Xen's microtonal tuning does not apply to VST3 instruments. They play in normal tuning.
  • On Windows, the plugin's own window does not open yet, so the button is not shown. Use the knobs in the rack.

Building GrooveForge yourself

The Steinberg VST3 SDK is not in the repository. Before building for Linux, macOS or Windows, clone it into packages/flutter_vst3/vst3sdk with its submodules. The command is in the README.
